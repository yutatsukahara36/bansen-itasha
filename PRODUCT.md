# デジタル番宣痛車 — PRODUCT

## What it is

A virtual 痛車 on a website. Japanese companies buy one of 40 fixed-price ad spots on its body (¥20,000 to ¥250,000, summing to exactly ¥2,000,000). Five extra spots are 非売品, given free to the first five ローンチパートナー companies. If all 40 sell, the car gets built for real. The real car is never promised: buyers purchase digital placement, and the site says so plainly.

The pitch in one line: 「アニメには番宣痛車があるのに、企業にはない。だから作る。」

## Who it is for

**Buyer:** the 中の人 running a playful Japanese corporate X account (シャープ, タニタ, キングジム, ヴィレッジヴァンガード, 井村屋, セガ, 小林製薬, ドン・キホーテ and roughly forty more). They are not buying impressions, they are buying a post they can make from their own account that no other company has. 35 of 40 spots sit under ¥100,000 so a 課長 can say yes without escalating.

**Scene sentence:** a 中の人 at a Japanese consumer brand, phone in hand on a commuter train in the morning, half-bored, deciding in four seconds whether this is funny enough to show their boss. Light theme, high contrast, legible at phone size in daylight glare.

**Audience:** everyone else who sees the link on X: 痛車 culture accounts (who will judge craft instantly), marketers (who like a clever mechanism), ねとらぼ / Togetter editors.

## Register

Brand. Design is the product. The car render is the product. A cheap-looking car kills the project regardless of strategy.

## Tone

店頭. Playful, toy-like retail. The car is merchandise on a shop floor, sold piece by piece. Village Vanguard's handwritten POP札 (black marker on yellow paper, taped crooked) is the visual signature. The car carries all the chaos; the page around it is a white gallery.

Copy: direct, light, honest. 「約束はできません。ここは正直に書きます。」 is the tone anchor. No 定型文, no throat-clearing, no em dashes.

## The one thing people remember

値札だらけの車. A car wearing 45 price tags instead of logos. This is the Phase 1 launch image and it exists before a single sponsor signs.

## The signature interaction

値札がめくれる. When a spot sells, its POP札 peels away and the sponsor logo is underneath. Every sale is a three-second video.

## Anti-references

- Million Dollar Toilet's green grid and SaaS metric overlay. We are a shop counter, not a dashboard.
- Anime-moe 痛車 aesthetics. This is corporate 痛車, the joke is the gap.
- Generic dark SaaS / glassmorphism / gradient hero. Materially wrong for paper and marker.
- Crypto anything. Conservative Japanese buyers bounce on the first whiff.
